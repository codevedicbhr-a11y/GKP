About Us

Know more about Global Knowledge Public School

A School with Vision & Heart

Global Knowledge Public School, located in Nirmali-05 (Supaul), is a progressive CBSE-pattern institution dedicated to providing quality education from Nursery to Class VIII.

We believe education is not just about textbooks — it is the ability to meet life's situations with confidence and character. Our campus is a vibrant space where students learn modern subjects while staying rooted in Indian culture, tradition and values.

With experienced educators and a caring environment, we help every child discover their potential. Established in 2023, we continue to grow as a trusted name in education in the region.
