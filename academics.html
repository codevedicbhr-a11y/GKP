Our Academic Programs

CBSE pattern curriculum from Nursery to Class VIII

Early Years (Nursery, LKG, UKG)

Play-based learning that builds curiosity, language skills and social confidence in a joyful environment.

  • Activity-based learning
  • Basic literacy & numeracy
  • Rhymes, stories & art
  • Safe and caring classroom

Primary (Class I – V)

Strong foundation in core subjects with value education and interactive teaching methods.

  • English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS
  • Moral science & life skills
  • Regular assessments
  • Co-curricular activities

Middle School (Class VI – VIII)

CBSE-pattern curriculum preparing students for higher studies with critical thinking and confidence.

  • Science, Social Science, Maths, Languages
  • Project-based learning
  • Exam preparation focus
  • Personality development

Cultural & Co-curricular

Regular celebrations of festivals, competitions and performances that keep students rooted in Indian tradition.

  • Independence Day, Republic Day
  • Janmashtami, Raksha Bandhan & other festivals
  • Sports and games
  • Drawing, music & dance
